General information and controller

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of information in the mobile application “RetroBoy” (hereinafter, the “App”), distributed via Google Play under identifier com.retroboy.emulator, developed independently without an associated legal entity. This policy is governed by the principles of data minimisation, purpose limitation and privacy by design (Art. 5 and 25 GDPR).

02

Collection and processing of personal data

RetroBoy applies the principle of zero personal data. There is no processing of personal data within the meaning of GDPR Art. 4(1), nor identifiers enabling re-identification.

  • ZERO COLLECTION OF PERSONAL DATA: The App does NOT collect, store, process or share any personally identifiable information (such as names, phone numbers, physical addresses, e-mails, accounts or financial data).
  • NO ACCOUNT CREATION: No registration, login or profile creation is required to use all emulator features.
  • NO SALE OF DATA: We do not sell, rent or trade any data with third parties or advertising networks.
In short: RetroBoy runs 100% locally and anonymously. There are no proprietary servers storing personal data.
03

Access to local files (ROMs and saves)

  • —The App requires read and write access only to files and folders the user voluntarily selects via the official secure Android picker (Storage Access Framework / SAF).
  • —Game files (ROMs with extensions .gb .gbc .gba .zip), save files (.sav .srm) and save states are processed and stored EXCLUSIVELY locally on your device.
  • —At no time are your ROM files or saves uploaded, copied or transmitted to external servers. The user is solely responsible for owning a legal copy of any title imported.
04

Internet connectivity and third-party services

The App declares the network permission (INTERNET) exclusively for the following opt-in features, disableable in Settings. No transmission occurs without explicit user consent.

A) COVER ART DOWNLOAD (BOX ARTS)

If enabled in settings, the app anonymously queries open community repositories (such as libretro-thumbnails on GitHub) to download cover art for the game title. No user information or unique device identifiers are sent in this request.

B) SUPPORT LINK AND ERROR REPORTING

Tapping “Report an Error or Suggestion” opens the user’s default mail client to send a voluntary message.

05

Advertising (if applicable)

If the version distributed via Google Play displays advertising, it is provided exclusively through Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may process the Advertising ID and basic device information to serve and measure ads, only after obtaining consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required (EEA/UK). You can choose between personalized and non-personalized ads and change your choice at any time in the app’s settings or in your device’s advertising settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

No personal data is sold. If you do not grant consent where it is required, no advertising requiring consent is shown. The developer has no access to your advertising profile and does not receive your personal data from the ad network.

Note: If your installed build is ad-free, this section does not apply. You can verify it in Settings → About within the app.
06

Device permissions

RetroBoy requests only the permissions strictly necessary for its correct technical operation:

VIBRATE
Haptic feedback when pressing on-screen buttons.
WAKE_LOCK
Keep screen on while playing.
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Thumbnail download and connectivity check.
07

Children’s privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

The App is directed to the general public and does not knowingly collect any information from children. As there is no account creation, tracking or processing of personal data, identification of minors is not possible, complying with COPPA and Art. 8 GDPR (GDPR-K parental consent).

08

Information security

As all game data, settings and saves remain exclusively on the device’s local storage, their confidentiality and integrity are protected by Android’s isolation, encryption and access-control mechanisms. There is no transmission to or custody on developer servers; therefore centralized breach notification (Art. 33-34 GDPR) does not apply beyond the security of the user’s own device.

For any questions regarding this Policy or RetroBoy’s operation, you may exercise your rights of access, rectification and erasure —limited in this case to the absence of processing— through the support channels indicated in the App’s Google Play listing.
